RAW Lab

Robust AI in the Wild · AI Thrust, HKUST (Guangzhou) · from 2027

We sit at the intersection of computer vision, computational photography, and physical AI — building systems that perceive, reconstruct, and interact with the physical world, grounded in the principles of light and imaging.

RAW is three things at once.

The signal
Unprocessed data straight from the camera sensor, before any pipeline touches it.
Robust AI in the Wild
Perception that holds up outside the conditions it was trained on.
本质
The Chinese word for essence — the world as it truly is, before any approximation is made.

Research Directions including but not limited to

Physics-Aware 3D Neural Representations

Physics-grounded neural representations for 3D reconstruction, rendering, and synthesis — closing the loop between vision and graphics.

Radiance Fields · 3D Gaussian Splatting · Inverse Rendering · Digital Twins

Robust Computer Vision

Visual perception that holds up when the world gets hard — across illumination, weather, and the full range of conditions cameras encounter in the wild.

Low-level Vision · ISP · Computational Photography

Embodied Perception

Designing vision systems from the ground up for agents that act in the physical world — where sensing, physics, and decision-making are co-designed as one.

Embodied AI · Hardware Co-design · Robustness

Physics-Guided World Models

Bringing physical laws into the generative process — where synthesis is not mere appearance but a consequence of how light and matter interact.

Simulation · Data Engines · World Models

Inverse Problems in Vision

The world leaves traces in images — we invert that process, recovering the hidden physical causes behind what cameras observe.

Inverse Problems · Scene Understanding · Remote Sensing

Evaluation Beyond Clean Data

Rethinking how we measure progress — benchmarks grounded in real sensor physics, where degradation is not noise to be removed but reality to be understood.

Benchmarks · 3D Reconstruction

Openings

RAW Lab is recruiting its first cohort. I am looking for people who are curious about how images come to exist, comfortable reading a paper and a datasheet in the same afternoon, and willing to spend time on problems that do not have clean benchmarks yet.

Fall 2027 Intake

Open positions for PhD students, Master students, and visiting students / research assistants. Prior experience in low-level vision, 3D reconstruction, or robotics is welcome but not required — a strong programming background and genuine interest matter more.

Email cui [at] mi.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp with your CV, transcript, and a short note on what you would like to work on.
